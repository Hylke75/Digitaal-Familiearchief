# Bewora Positioning

## Category
Bewora aims to own a category that is currently poorly defined:

**An automatic independent archive of your existing digital life.**

The important distinction is that the user does not start by uploading everything manually. They connect the services they already use and Bewora preserves an independent archive.

## Primary problem
Family photos, videos, social posts and important documents are spread across:
- phones
- cloud drives
- photo services
- social platforms
- old accounts

Every platform preserves its own ecosystem.

The user's life is fragmented across them.

## Mechanism
**Connect once.**

Bewora should make the user journey:
1. Choose a source.
2. Authenticate with the provider.
3. Give permission.
4. Bewora imports the accessible existing history.
5. Bewora continues preserving new content where the provider technically allows it.
6. The user normally does not have to think about it again.

## Outcome
The user gets an independent archive that can be searched, viewed, exported and eventually passed on.

## Primary audience
Lead with the family/household perspective.

The hero should not attempt to serve every audience at once.

## Secondary audiences
### Family preservation
People thinking increasingly about preserving family history for children and later generations.

### Digital self-determination
People motivated by ownership, privacy, portability and independence from platforms.

## Main message
**Instagram bewaart Instagram. Google bewaart Google. Bewora bewaart jou.**

Supporting message:
> Koppel je foto's, sociale media en documenten één keer. Bewora verzamelt ze automatisch in een onafhankelijk digitaal archief dat van jou blijft.

## Four-word product model
**Koppelen → Bewaren → Terugvinden → Doorgeven**

## Why Bewora is not Dropbox
Dropbox provides a place where the user deliberately stores files.

Bewora is designed to gather existing content from multiple services and preserve an independent archive.

## Why Bewora is not only backup
The long-term product experience includes:
- timeline
- people
- places
- events
- documents
- family archive
- search
- portability

The archive should become usable and meaningful, not merely restorable.

## Why Bewora is not a legacy vault
Legacy is a later outcome.

The everyday reason to use Bewora is:
- preserve now
- simplify now
- find memories now
- remain independent now

Do not position the brand primarily around death.

## Strongest objection
**What if Bewora itself stops?**

The minimum credible answer is portability.

Bewora must be designed so the user can export:
- original files
- portable metadata
- standard formats

Do not promise eternal storage or post-bankruptcy continuity unless a legally and financially valid mechanism exists.

## Trust model
Until Bewora has social proof, trust should be created through:
- real product screens
- transparent capabilities
- clear privacy policy
- clear security page
- export
- accurate provider explanations
- clear company/contact details when verified

Do not fabricate testimonials or statistics.

9. Family section

13. FAQ
14. Final CTA
15. Complete footer

## Product proof
The homepage must show the actual product.

No empty decorative placeholder where the product should be.

Where practical, render real Bewora UI components with synthetic demo data rather than stale screenshots.

## Pricing
Do not hide pricing indefinitely.

However, no price may be invented.

Pricing must be driven from a central configuration once approved.
