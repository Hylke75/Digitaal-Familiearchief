# Bewora Brand Guidelines

## Brand
**Bewora**

## Brand promise
**Bewaar wat van jou is.**

International:
**Keep what’s yours.**

## Category
Bewora is an independent automatic digital-life archive.

It is not positioned primarily as:
- cloud storage
- backup software
- a password manager
- a digital inheritance vault
- a cybersecurity product

## Core proposition
**Instagram bewaart Instagram. Google bewaart Google. Bewora bewaart jou.**

Supporting copy:
> Koppel je foto's, sociale media en documenten één keer. Bewora verzamelt ze automatisch in een onafhankelijk digitaal archief dat van jou blijft.

## Product mechanism
**Koppelen → Bewaren → Terugvinden → Doorgeven**

## Primary audience
The primary homepage audience is a parent/household with years of family photos, videos, social content and documents spread across multiple services and devices.

Do not state an age range in marketing copy.

## Secondary motivations
1. Family preservation — passing family memories and context to future generations.
2. Digital independence — ownership, exportability and independence from technology platforms.

## Brand personality
Bewora should feel:
- calm
- warm
- trustworthy
- premium
- human
- independent
- concrete
- non-technical

Bewora should not feel:
- cold
- corporate
- cybersecurity-heavy
- eco/wellness
- death-first
- generic SaaS
- generic cloud storage

## Colour palette

### Archive Green
`#1D3D2A`

Primary brand colour. Use for primary buttons, logo, important navigation states and selected dark brand panels.

### Archive Green Dark
`#142C1E`

Hover/darker supporting state.

### Warm Paper
`#FAF6F1`

Primary warm background.

### Surface
`#FFFFFF`

Cards and elevated surfaces.

### Brass
`#9B5B19`

Use sparingly for premium details, timeline markers and ownership/export accents.

### Charcoal Green
`#1B211D`

Primary text.

### Secondary Text
`#59635C`

Secondary body text.

### Sage
`#A8B4A9`

Muted support colour.

### Warning Text
`#8A4B12`

Do not use the old warning colour `#C67A19` for normal text.

### Interactive Border
Use a validated colour around `#7F8981` for form boundaries where a 3:1 non-text contrast is required.

Decorative card borders may remain lighter.

## Typography
Use the current high-quality sans-serif if suitable. Otherwise use Inter.

Tone:
- editorial
- modern
- highly readable
- calm
- confident

Avoid playful, futuristic or luxury-fashion typography.

## Logo direction
Retain the name **Bewora** and a recognisable stylised **B**.

The previous logo reference is only a starting point.

The production mark should reduce the botanical/leaf interpretation and move toward:
- pages
- layers
- preserved memories
- open archive
- subtle open book
- continuity/time

The mark should ideally read as several things at once:
- B
- two pages
- two archive layers
- an open story

Avoid:
- leaves as the dominant interpretation
- padlocks
- shields
- clouds
- folders
- hard drives
- database icons
- generic infinity marks

## Visual motif
Use **time / timeline** as a recurring visual motif.

Example:

```text
2011 ─── ●
2015 ─── ●
2020 ─── ●
2026 ─── ●
```

Use sparingly in homepage sections, My Life, marketing visuals and brand material.

## Photography
Photos should feel real, European, warm and personal.

Prioritise:
- families
- ordinary moments
- children growing up
- parents and grandparents
- holidays
- birthdays
- home life
- old photographs alongside recent phone photography

Avoid a site dominated by anonymous travel landscapes or obvious corporate stock photography.

## Tone of voice
Use Dutch “je”, not “u”.

Tone:
- short
- calm
- specific
- human
- reassuring

Avoid:
- hype
- fear marketing
- excessive exclamation marks
- superlatives
- excessive AI messaging
- technical jargon

## Core copy
### Primary homepage statement
**Instagram bewaart Instagram. Google bewaart Google. Bewora bewaart jou.**

### Brand line
**Bewaar wat van jou is.**

### International
**Keep what’s yours.**

### Secondary legacy/family line
**Het fotoalbum van vroeger. Maar dan voor je hele digitale leven.**

## Ownership proof
Export is part of the brand promise, not merely a feature.

Where the product supports it, show visibly:
**Download mijn volledige archief**

Users must be able to understand that their archive is portable and not locked into Bewora.

## Trust rules
Never invent:
- testimonials
- ratings
- certifications
- user numbers
- media coverage
- security audit results
- company details
- server locations

Only render verified trust facts.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Do not rely on colour alone for status.

Validate:
- text contrast
- form boundaries
- focus states
- links
- warning states
- buttons
